A deserted, dusty, silent Victorian carriage. The ceiling is covered with thousands of small tally marks scratched at regular intervals. In the last compartment, two figures: a gaunt old man with wild white hair and a greedy smile, and a thin terrified clerk with messy hair. Through the windows, a grey world like a fogged photographic plate, crossed by countless blue-black trails coiled like a gigantic serpent. The only red: a twisted figure-eight pattern painted on the floor.

Black and white orthochromatic film, high contrast, silver grain, faint scratches and dust, soft vignette, slightly uneven exposure. Only one element in saturated deep red; everything else in black, white and grey. Photographic realism, cinematic, eerie, quiet dread, 1923. Panoramic cinema frame, 2.4:1, composed for its full width: use the width for the set, the shadows and the depth.
